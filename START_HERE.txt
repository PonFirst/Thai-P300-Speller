THAI P300 SPELLER FOR WINDOWS
=============================

You need: a Windows 10 or 11 laptop, the Unicorn Hybrid Black headset (charged),
and the file ThaiSpeller-Windows.zip.


SET UP (once, about 5 minutes)
------------------------------

1. Unblock the download.
   Right-click ThaiSpeller-Windows.zip > Properties > tick "Unblock" at the bottom > OK.
   (No Unblock box? Skip this step.)

2. Unzip it to C:\
   Right-click the zip > Extract All... > change the folder to   C:\   > Extract.
   You now have the folder C:\ThaiSpeller.

3. Pair the headset.
   Switch the headset on.
   Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Add device > Bluetooth > choose UN-...

Nothing else to install.


EACH SESSION
------------

1. Prepare the room and laptop.
   - Unplug the laptop charger (it adds electrical noise to the EEG).
   - Display at 60 Hz: Settings > System > Display > Advanced display > refresh rate 60 Hz.
   - Quiet room, phone silenced.

2. Put the headset on.
   - Gel in all 8 electrodes.
   - Both sticker electrodes on the bone behind the ears, cables clipped in.

3. Double-click  C:\ThaiSpeller\ThaiSpeller.cmd
   - "Windows protected your PC"?  Click More info > Run anyway.
   - Windows Firewall asks about BCI2000 or Python?  Click Allow.
     (The programs only talk to each other on this laptop.)

4. Answer the questions: Y for the real headset, the participant code
   (letters and digits, e.g. S01, never a name), and the session number.

5. Do the steps in order. Type the step's number and press Enter.
   1  Look at the signal   Check the 8 traces look calm. Close the Operator window when done.
   2  Check run            Follow the instructions. All four result lines must say PASS.
   3  Calibration, part 1  Press Start in the Operator window.
   4  Calibration, part 2  Rest a few minutes first.
   5  Fit                  The AUC it prints should be about 0.70 or more.
   6  Spelling, all flashes
   7  Spelling, dynamic stopping
   8  Reports              Opens the results in your web browser.
   Finished steps are marked "done". Type 0 to quit.

During calibration and spelling, the participant looks at the highlighted letter,
silently counts how often it flashes, and blinks only between letters.


WHERE THE DATA GOES
-------------------

Recordings, logs and reports:  C:\ThaiSpeller\collection\recordings\bci2000\<code><session>\
The participant's model:       C:\ThaiSpeller\models\
To back up, copy both folders.


TRY IT WITHOUT THE HEADSET
--------------------------

Start ThaiSpeller.cmd and type R instead of Y. A fake headset sends made-up signals,
and its files go to collection\recordings\bci2000_rehearsal.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------

"No paired headset found"     Switch the headset on and pair it (set up, step 3).
"the headset sent nothing"    Switch the headset off for 10 seconds, on again, and repeat the step.
All channels look wild        Unplug the charger, press the stickers behind the ears, add gel.
"BCI2000 is already running"  Close the Operator window first.
Anything else                 Send the text in the window and the run's .system.log file
                              (in the recordings folder) to the person who gave you this package.


UPDATING
--------

Extract the new zip to C:\ the same way, then copy your old collection\recordings and
models folders into the new C:\ThaiSpeller.
